# OpenH3 Code of Conduct

OpenH3 is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone who uses, contributes to, or discusses the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or hate speech.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated off-topic promotion.
- Sharing credentials, provider keys, private URLs, or other sensitive data.
- Any conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Please report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the GitHub profile for [@pigq](https://github.com/pigq). Do not create a public issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
